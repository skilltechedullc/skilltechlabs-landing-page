# Skilltech Labs

> Skilltech Labs is an AI product company based in the UAE, building production-ready SaaS platforms and autonomous AI systems from the ground up. Every product ships with AI built in — not bolted on. The company is operated by SKILLTECH EDUCATION AND TRAINING COMPUTER SOFTWARE L.L.C.

Skilltech Labs builds infrastructure-level software that replaces entire workflows — not just automates tasks. Products span business automation, ecommerce, fleet management, HR, fintech, career tools, and parental monitoring. The flagship product, AI OS, is a fully autonomous AI-powered business operating system with ten specialized agents running 24/7.

## Products

- [AI OS](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/ai-os): Fully autonomous AI-powered business operating system. Ten AI agents replacing marketing, sales, leads, CRM, research, operations, and account management — 24/7, zero salaries.
- [CVBuddy](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/cvbuddy): WhatsApp-based AI CV generation system powered by Claude AI. Users send details conversationally and receive a polished, professional CV instantly. No subscriptions — pay per result.
- [Ecommerce Engine](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/ecommerce-engine): Production-ready single-tenant ecommerce platform. Launch a full online store in minutes. Integrated WhatsApp shopping, AI product search, automated last-mile delivery handoff. Live tenant: shop.millco.in
- [FleetScan](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/fleetscan): Multi-tenant car rental SaaS platform. Bookings, fleet tracking, vehicle management, and smart operational insights in one system.
- [CardFlow](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/cardflow): Multi-POS transaction management SaaS. One dashboard for every point-of-sale, every transaction, every location — built for businesses running multiple payment points.
- [Recruitment ATS](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/recruitment-ats): Scalable multi-tenant applicant tracking system. Hire smarter, track every applicant across the full pipeline.
- [AngelEye](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/angeleye): Parental monitoring platform for tracking and protecting children's digital activity.
- [WhatsApp English](https://skilltechlabs.com/products/whatsapp-english): Hybrid AI-powered English learning system delivered entirely through WhatsApp. Designed for real people, built for real results.
